# Heaven Behind The Mountain Pass

*Expected Release: December 2027*

## Synopsis

In the remote, snow-capped peaks of Kashmir, magic isn't something grand or earth-shattering—it is quiet, hidden in the mundane rituals of brewing tea and the careful tending of overgrown gardens. 

**Heaven Behind The Mountain Pass** follows the journey of unlikely companions who must navigate the treacherous, avalanche-prone valleys to uncover a long-lost truth about their ancestry. But as the winter closes in, they soon realize that the true magic lies not in the artifacts they seek, but in the bonds they forge along the way. 

It is a story about the quiet corners of magic, the profound importance of found family, and the hardest journey of all: learning to love ourselves.

## What to Expect

- **Atmospheric Setting:** A richly imagined, cozy fantasy set against the breathtaking backdrop of Kashmir.
- **Deep Character Arcs:** A character-driven narrative focusing on personal growth and healing.
- **Subtle Magic System:** A world where magic is woven into everyday life rather than epic battles.
